# Surface brief: landing page (app/page.tsx) and sign-in screens

Mode: Persuade. Audience: first-time creators arriving on laptop or phone. Job: understand Aether Studio in one scroll and start creating free. Constraints from the client: a space theme, and a robot who explains everything. Copy is the existing product copy; Aiko's guide lines restate it. The brief was inferred from the request and the existing site (the owner was asleep; no interview was run).

## Direction contract

THESIS: one guided flight. Aiko, the product's assistant rebuilt as a hovering robot, stays on screen the whole way and flies stop to stop as you scroll. Refuses the category default: violet-glow hero, label over every heading, nine equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: near-black void with a drifting nebula and stars you fly through. Warm white display type (Bricolage Grotesque). Amber is the only action colour; violet belongs to Aiko's light and the sky; coral is her eyes. Round shapes: pills, soft panels, hairline rules instead of cards.

STORY: the visitor meets Aiko, watches the generator work, learns the numbers, reads nine features as three chapters each over its own planet, sees Director Mode relight the scene, meets Aiko properly, sees the stack in orbit, then falls toward a black hole into the call to action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left, "Create" over a cycling amber word at about 6rem, one paragraph, amber primary button and ghost secondary. Right, Aiko at roughly 60% of viewport height, waving, head tracking the cursor, speech bubble by her head. Route rail at the left edge.

FORM: code-led WebGL scroll flight with a persistent 3D guide; signature interaction is Aiko flying between stops and speaking at each. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished.
