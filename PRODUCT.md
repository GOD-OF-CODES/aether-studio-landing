# Aether Studio

Written from the existing app and the brief for this rebuild. Lines marked *assumed* were not confirmed by the owner.

## What it is
Aether Studio is an AI image studio built by Yash Pandey. You describe a picture, pick a style and aspect ratio, and it generates the image. Aiko, the built-in assistant, improves prompts, analyses finished images and answers art questions from a knowledge base of 40+ entries.

## What makes it different
- **Director Mode:** six one-tap cinematic looks (Nolan, Ghibli, Blade Runner, Kubrick, Wong Kar-wai, Villeneuve), each a fixed prompt recipe.
- **Aiko:** a consistent character who chats, rewrites prompts and gives feedback on lighting, colour and composition.
- **Finishing tools in the same place:** 4× upscaling, crop and resize, background removal, cloud history.

## Who it is for
Hobbyist and independent creators making images for fun, wallpapers and personal projects. *Assumed:* they arrive on a laptop or phone in the evening, curious, with no account yet.

## What the landing page must do
Get a first-time visitor to understand the studio in one scroll and start creating for free. The client asked for a space theme and a robot who explains everything.

## Voice
Warm, plain, a little playful. Aiko speaks in the first person in short lines. No hype words beyond the product's own copy.

## Facts and limits
- Free to use.
- Built on Cloudflare AI, Groq (Llama 3.3), Gemini Vision, Firebase, Supabase and Flutter.
- The old page showed "1247 images generated today" (hardcoded) and "Join thousands of creators" (unverified). Neither is used as a claim on the new page.
